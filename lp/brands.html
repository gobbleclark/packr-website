FAQ

What your brands get

What can a brand see?

Its own orders, holds, backorders, inbound shipments, shipping errors and SLA, live from your WMS. Never another brand's. Your team can still keep internal tasks and threads the brand doesn't see.

We ship for dozens of brands. Does each one get its own space?

Yes. Every brand gets its own channel with its own contacts and SLA. Brands and users are unlimited on every plan, so adding your next account never changes the bill.

How long does setup take?

Connecting is instant: just your WMS login. A background job then pulls in your full order history, which can take around 7 hours. You can start working in Packr the moment you connect. Start your 30-day free trial →

Which WMS do you integrate with?

Today we integrate with ShipHero, with a deep two-way sync of orders, holds, shipments and errors. More WMS integrations are in development, so if you're on a different platform, talk to us.

What does it cost?

One usage-based plan: $8 per 1,000 orders, with unlimited brands, unlimited users and every feature included. No contracts and no setup fees. Size it for your volume →
