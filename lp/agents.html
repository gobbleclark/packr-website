FAQ

About Pax

Can Pax actually make changes to orders?

Yes. Pax can manage holds, change addresses and carriers, edit line items, escalate, cancel, and record or resolve shipping errors, all against your live WMS data.

Does Pax act on its own?

No. Pax proposes and a person on your team approves. Every change is a card you click, and agents park their work for your approval before anything is written to the WMS.

What does Pax know about my operation?

Your orders, holds, inventory, inbound shipments, shipping errors and SLA, pulled live from your WMS and scoped to what the person asking is allowed to see.

Which WMS do you integrate with?

Today we integrate with ShipHero, with a deep two-way sync of orders, holds, shipments and errors. More WMS integrations are in development, so if you're on a different platform, talk to us.

What does Pax cost?

Pax is included. One usage-based plan: $8 per 1,000 orders, with unlimited brands, unlimited users and every feature. No contracts and no setup fees. Size it for your volume →
