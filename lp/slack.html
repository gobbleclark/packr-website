FAQ

Before you switch

How is Packr different from Slack Connect?

Conversations in Packr live next to the order data they're about, pulled live from your WMS. Every request can become a task with an owner and an SLA, and order edits write back to the warehouse. Nothing gets lost in a fourth Slack workspace or a forwarded email.

Will our brands have to learn a new tool?

It works like the chat they already use: channels, threads, mentions and reactions, on the web and on iOS and Android. The difference is that their orders sit right beside the conversation.

How long does setup take?

Connecting is instant: just your WMS login. A background job then pulls in your full order history, which can take around 7 hours. You can start working in Packr the moment you connect. Start your 30-day free trial →

Which WMS do you integrate with?

Today we integrate with ShipHero, with a deep two-way sync of orders, holds, shipments and errors. More WMS integrations are in development, so if you're on a different platform, talk to us.

What does it cost?

One usage-based plan: $8 per 1,000 orders, with unlimited brands, unlimited users and every feature included. No contracts and no setup fees. Size it for your volume →
